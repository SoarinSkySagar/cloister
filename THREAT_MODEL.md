# Cloister threat model

Written 19 Nov 2026 against circuit v2 (`circuits/ledger`), the sequencer, relayer, wallet, watchtower and receipts tools in this repository. Each claim names the test or on-chain evidence behind it; where there is none, it says so. The circuit's constraints have **not yet had a human review** (see [docs/circuit-review.md](docs/circuit-review.md)). Known limits are collected in [LIMITS.md](LIMITS.md).

## 1. System and trust boundaries

| Component | Role | Trusted for |
|---|---|---|
| **QoreChain (L1)** | Verifies the Groth16 proof, stores batches and the verifying key, enforces that private posts are envelopes for the member set, pays withdrawals from escrow, anchors each batch with the creator's ML-DSA-87 signature | Correct verification; honest governance (only governance can change the key after batch 0) |
| **Sequencer / prover** (rollup creator, a member) | Decrypts posts, checks signatures, orders and executes transactions, proves batches, posts and submits them | Ordering and inclusion; honest signature checking (not proven by the circuit) |
| **Relayer** | Public door: validates, rate-limits and forwards signed transactions; paid in-rollup from fees | Availability only; it never holds keys and sees only ciphertext once sealed |
| **Member watchtowers** | Decrypt every batch, re-check structure, hash link, every ML-DSA signature and every signed time window, sign a verdict | At least one honest member |
| **Users** | Sign frame transactions (sender, co-signers, sponsor) with ML-DSA-87 | Their own keys |
| **Verifier (anyone)** | Checks receipts, verdicts and inclusion proofs against live chain state | Nothing: every check re-reads the chain |

Privacy model: **members see everything; outsiders see ciphertext, roots and proofs.** To outsiders Cloister behaves like a validium: they can verify each state transition was valid but cannot rebuild the state. This is a consortium model, not privacy from the sequencer.

## 2. Assets

Balances · transaction contents · who paid and who co-signed · escrow and withdrawal rights · the verifying key · key material (sender, party, sponsor, member ML-KEM keys).

## 3. Adversaries

Malicious sequencer · malicious relayer · malicious sponsor · malicious co-signer / counterparty · outside observer · colluding members · quantum adversary (Q-Day) and holders of leaked classical keys · governance (key change).

## 4. What the circuit proves, and what it does not

**Proven on chain** (a Groth16 proof QoreChain verified; v2 first verified 19 Nov, see PROOF.md): every state change is a valid move on the committed tree; balances stay in `[0, 2^64)`; value is conserved per asset except withdrawals; only QOR leaves, and each withdrawal leaf the chain pays is backed by a debit of the same amount; nonces move once per included tx; a key is only set on a key-less account; **an applied action debits only the sender or a listed co-signer**; **every included tx is inside its time window** against an in-state batch counter; **the batch's transaction records, with co-signers and window, are committed in `state_root`**. Circuit v3 (22 Nov; **verified by QoreChain on `cloister-dev-5`, batches 0 to 7, 23 Nov**) adds: **a skipped action must be one that could not apply** (insufficient balance, a credit past 2^64, full withdrawal slots, a key already set; judged on the group's tentative balances); **each record carries a digest of what the signers wrote** (planned actions including skipped ones, window, co-signers, payer, max fee, signed sender, chain and rollup); **the fee is at most the signed max fee**; **a new account must be an empty leaf and register a key**.

**Not proven** (checked off-circuit, so detected rather than prevented): ML-DSA signatures of the sender, co-signers and sponsor (this is the gap that lets a forged party list or window stand, as far as the circuit goes; the digest makes the lie detectable by anyone holding the signed transaction); that executed transactions are those in the sealed post (the watchtower's `--replay` re-execution checks this); ordering and censorship.

## 5. Threats and mitigations

| # | Threat | Mitigation | Residual |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Q-Day**: a classical key is broken | Rollup transactions are ML-DSA-87 only; every L1 transaction is hybrid (secp256k1 + ML-DSA-87); data sealed with ML-KEM-1024 | Hash-based or classical parts of the L1 are QoreChain's |
| T2 | **Forged transaction** (the circuit does not verify ML-DSA) | Sequencer checks every signature; every member watchtower re-checks them after decrypting (`signature` check) and signs a verdict | Detected, not prevented on chain. Single honest-member assumption |
| T3 | **Forged co-signer**: a sequencer lists an account as a party without its signature and debits it | Watchtower verifies **every** party signature; the party list is committed in the record **and in its digest**, so an inclusion proof shows who was named and a list the signers did not write does not match the signed transaction (`check-tx`); `--replay` re-execution exposes a list the sealed bundle does not hold; a party only loses what the signed actions say | A malicious sequencer with no honest watchtower can debit any listed account. Same trust as T2, with more accounts exposed per tx |
| T4 | **Co-signer griefing**: a party signs a transaction that debits it unexpectedly | The signature covers the whole transaction, every leg and amount; atomic legs apply all-or-nothing; only the sender's nonce moves, so the tx runs at most once | A party who signs a bad deal has signed it |
| T5 | **Fee redirected** to an account the sequencer controls | The relayer account is a constant of the circuit and every fee must pay it (negative test, mutation-checked); the record also shows the recipient | — (fixed 20 Nov, finding F1) |
| T6 | **Censorship of an action**: sequencer skips a valid action and still charges the fee | **Circuit v3 rejects it**: a skip needs a failing action in its atomic group (rows S1 to S10, negative tests, mutation-tested). The record and `check-tx` show which actions applied | Demonstrated on chain: `cloister-dev-5` batch 4 skipped a swap whose second leg could not apply and the proof verified (PROOF.md). Not enforced on circuits before v3 (`cloister-dev-3`). The sequencer still chooses which transactions to include and in what order (T8). A skip claimed with false planned terms is caught by the digest, not the circuit (F2) |
| T7 | **Wider window than signed**: sequencer claims `not_before = 0` for a tx that signed 100 | The window is in the record's digest, so the lie changes the digest; `check-tx`, the `window` check and `--replay` re-execution detect it | Detection, not prevention: the circuit does not verify the signature that covers the window (F3, `docs/pq-auth.md`) |
| T8 | **Sequencer censors or reorders** | Member watchtower; based inbox if the final rollup is based; users can prove what was included | Single sequencer. Dedicated rollups let only the sequencer post |
| T9 | **Relayer drops or snoops** | Relayer sees only a transaction a user chose to send it; it cannot sign; refuses malformed, oversized, under-fee and rate-limited txs | A dropping relayer is an availability problem; use another or post via the sequencer |
| T10 | **Sponsor drained** by spam | `max_fee` is signed; the fee is flat; the relayer enforces a fee floor and a rate limit; the sponsor signs every tx it pays for | A sponsor pays for txs that fail their actions (the fee is still charged); cap exposure with `max_fee` |
| T11 | **Replay** | Nonce increments once per included tx; expiry and `not_before` in the signed tx; chain id and rollup id are in the signature hash | — |
| T12 | **Time-lock bypass** | Circuit enforces `not_before <= n <= expiry` against the batch counter (negative tests, boundary tests); the sequencer holds an early tx | The record's window is a witness (T7) |
| T13 | **Verifying-key change** | The key locks after batch 0; only governance can change it; the committed key is in `circuits/setup/<id>` | Governance trust. Watchtower alarm for key updates: **not built** |
| T14 | **Data withholding** | Watchtower opens every envelope in range; members only | Members only |
| T15 | **Escrow-backed balances** (no deposit path on `defi` rollups) | Account 0 holds the escrow at genesis; value is conserved, so total QOR never exceeds the escrow | Operator-credited onboarding: trust the treasury key (F5) |
| T16 | **Disclosure through proofs** | An inclusion proof reveals one record (sender, co-signers, window, fee payer, actions) and nothing else in the batch; the sequencer only serves it to accounts the record names (sender, co-signers, fee payer) via a signed query | Whoever receives a proof sees that record. Single-field disclosure is not supported |
| T17 | **Metadata leakage** | Contents are sealed; the relayer hides who posted; the sequencer pads the sealed bundle (`--pad bucket|fixed`, spec/frame-tx.md) so the post's size does not reveal the transaction count or sizes (bucket) or anything (fixed) | Timing and cadence of batches, the member list, each batch's `tx_count`, the batch roots and every withdrawal (recipient and amount) are public on L1. Padding applies from the batch it is switched on; earlier posts keep their size |
| T18 | **Colluding members sign a false verdict** | A verdict is an off-chain signature by a current member whose key is read from the chain; a false `pass` is attributable | Accountability, not certainty; at least one honest member is assumed. No staking or slashing |
| T19 | **Removed member keeps old data** | New membership epoch on every change | Past data stays readable to them |
| T20 | **Trusted setup** | `ledger ceremony` runs a multi-party setup (Groth16 phase 1 and 2 with proofs of knowledge, a public beacon, a hash transcript; `docs/ceremony.md`; tests refuse skipped, repeated, reordered, restarted and corrupted chains) | Dev rollups still use the single-party `ledger setup`. The ceremony has been rehearsed, not run for a real key, and needs at least one honest contributor. The proof system is not post-quantum either way |
| T21 | **Reserved-leaf abuse**: moving value into or overwriting the log leaf | Circuit forbids any account update at the reserved index; checked old-leaf shape (negative tests, each constraint mutation-tested) | — |
| T22 | **Proof moved to another batch** | The batch number is inside the log leaf the proof hashes to (negative test) | — |
| T23 | **Executed is not what was sealed**: the sequencer proves a batch other than the posted bundle (drops, substitutes or reorders a transaction) | The watchtower's `--replay` re-executes every decrypted bundle from genesis through the prover's own builder and compares the state root with the chain's (4 negative tests: dropped, reordered, extra and altered transactions all differ) | Circuit v3 rollups only; needs `go`; a member sees all data, so only a member can run it. Run against `cloister-dev-5` batches 0 to 7 on 23 Nov: every replayed root equals the chain's |
| T25 | **Crash corrupts local state**: a crash or power cut mid-write leaves a truncated `state.json`, `keys.json` or `mempool.json` | Every state write goes to a temporary file, is synced and renamed (`packages/sequencer/src/atomic.ts`; the prover's own outputs likewise); a kill-the-writer test (30 kills against 8 MB files) never finds a truncated or mixed file, and a control shows a plain write would | The posted-but-not-submitted crash (data posted, batch not submitted) is untested on chain (`docs/decentralisation.md`) |
| T26 | **Silent operational failure**: a stalled sequencer, a verification-key change, a batch that does not continue the last, members changed, watchtowers disagreeing | `watchtower --alerts` (`src/alerts.ts`, 6 tests on fixtures); exit code 1 on a critical alert | The chain's rollup view exposes only whether a key exists, so the key alert reads the `rdk_verification_key_set` event (hash, who set it); verified live for a creation, but a change by governance has not been observed, so that path is fixture-tested only |
| T24 | **Account capture**: the sequencer assigns a registering user an account index that already holds funds | A transaction signed as a new account must run on an empty leaf (rows N1, N2; negative test) | — |

## 6. Review status

- Every circuit rule has a negative test, and each constraint was removed in turn to confirm a test fails without it (`circuits/mutation_check.py`: **70 of 70 caught**, 22 Nov, circuit v3; 37 of 37 on 20 Nov for v2). The older v1 rules did not pass this at first (11 were not isolated); an adversarial witness builder (`circuits/ledger/adversary_test.go`, field arithmetic with every check off) was written to test them properly, and two of those rules (key overwrite, inactive tx) now have tests that demonstrate the attack they prevent. One unreachable v2 constraint was removed.
- A full Groth16 round trip passes (compile, setup, prove, verify; four public inputs, no commitment key), and the v2 circuit's proofs were verified by QoreChain on `cloister-dev-3` (batches 0 to 6).
- A randomised differential test (`TestFuzzHonestBatchesAgreeWithTheReferenceAndTheCircuit`) runs random batches through the builder, an independent reference interpreter and the circuit, and attacks every applied last action by skipping it in the adversarial builder (F2).
- Not yet done: a human review of the constraints, and an independent look at the Poseidon2 construction. My own analysis of the leaf and digest constructions is in `docs/circuit-review.md` (F4); it is reasoning, not a proof.
